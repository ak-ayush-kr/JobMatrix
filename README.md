<h1 align="center">JobMatrix</h1>

<p align="center">
  A full-stack job portal connecting job seekers, recruiters, and companies.
</p>
<p> JobMatrix is a full-stack job portal that connects job seekers, recruiters, and companies on a single platform. It provides job discovery,
  applications, recruitment management, and intelligent job matching.</p>

<h2>🚀 Features</h2>

<h4>1. User Authentication</h4>
<p>
  Provides secure user registration and login with JWT-based authentication.
Different user roles are supported for job seekers and recruiters..
</p>

<h4>2. Job Management</h4>
<p>
  Recruiters can create, update, and manage job postings with complete job details.
  Job seekers can browse and explore available opportunities.
</p>

<h4>3. Job Applications</h4>
<p>
  Job seekers can apply for suitable jobs and track their application status.
  Recruiters can view and manage applications received for their postings.
</p>

<h4>4. AI-Based Job Matching</h4>
<p>
  Uses AI-generated embeddings to compare candidate skills with job requirements.
  Helps recruiters identify relevant candidates based on skill similarity.
</p>

<h4>5. Company Management</h4>
<p>
  Recruiters can create and manage company profiles associated with their jobs.
  Users can explore company information while searching for opportunities.
</p>

<h4>6. Real-Time Notification</h4>
<p>
  Uses Socket.IO for real-time communication and updates.
  The platform also supports real-time notification service.
</p>

<h4>7. Resume & Profile Management</h4>
<p>
  Users can manage their profiles, skills, resumes, and professional information.
  Cloud-based storage is used for handling profile and resume files.
</p>

<h4>8. Search & Job Discovery</h4>
<p>
  Users can search and explore job opportunities based on their requirements.
  The interface provides a simple and responsive job discovery experience.
</p>

<h2> -> Tech Stack</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Frontend:</strong> React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Redux Toolkit, React Router, Axios</li>
  <li><strong>Backend:</strong> Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Mongoose</li>
  <li><strong>Authentication:</strong> JWT</li>
  <li><strong>Real-Time:</strong> Socket.IO</li>
  <li><strong>Storage:</strong> Cloudinary</li>
  <li><strong>AI:</strong> Google GenAI, Embeddings</li>
  <li><strong>Email:</strong> Nodemailer</li>
</ul>
